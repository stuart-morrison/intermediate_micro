#  Intermediate Microeconomics: Imperfect Competition, Markets & Welfare Economics

Welcome! 

The goal of this course is to teach you how to analyze the world through the lens of imperfect competition. 

In particular, we'll focus on two broad themes:

- How market power prevails in society and makes us worse of
- How strategic thinking can help us excel, but also can lead us to deleterious social outcomes

## Topics

The topics we'll cover are:

- A refresher on competitive markets from ARE100A
- The other bookend - Monopolies and why they're the worst case scenario
- An introduction to game theory, that will provide the ability to think strategically about situations
- Using game theory to have more realistic models of market competition
- If we have time, some quick topics in modern competition: bundling and lock in
